Initial release of Emberbyte, a data-usage monitor that stays on your phone:
• Today's usage in large type, with a run-out forecast
• 12 themes in light, dark and AMOLED black
• Flexible plans, mid-cycle start, threshold alerts, per-app budgets
• Four home-screen widgets and a live notification
• Predictive back, real app icons, accessibility built in
• Encrypted backup and CSV/JSON export
• No INTERNET permission, no tracking, GPL-3.0
