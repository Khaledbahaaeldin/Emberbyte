Emberbyte, a data-usage monitor: 1.89GB
Counted.
Never shared.

Emberbyte shows what your phone uses, plans your month and warns you before you run out. Everything is measured and kept on the device. Nothing is ever sent anywhere.

Made to be read at a glance

One big number for today, and everything else one tap away.

◔

Plans that fit real life

Monthly or every-N-days cycles, SIM binding, add-on packs, free-data windows and rollover. Joined a plan mid-cycle? Enter what you already used, or start counting from the next cycle, and pin the plan you care about most.

⌁

A forecast you can trust

Learns your hourly pattern over 28 days and tells you the day you would run out.

◐

Alerts you choose

At 50, 80, 90 or 100 percent per plan, per-app budgets, and a spike detector.

▦

Four widgets, same theme as the app

Today, Plan, Bento and Dashboard. They show the same numbers as the app and follow your theme.

◈

Twelve themes, try them here

Dusk, Ember, Monochrome and nine community palettes, each with light, dark and pure black. Pick one: this page changes with it.

✦

Built for everyone

Large text without clipping, right-to-left layouts, TalkBack descriptions and reduced-motion support. Real app icons, smooth predictive back, and an encrypted backup you can restore on a new phone.

Privacy you can check

Do not take our word for it. Look at what the app is allowed to do.

  • ✓
    No internet permission. Without it, Android does not let an app send anything over the network. Your usage never leaves the phone.
  • ✓
    No accounts, no analytics, no trackers. There is nothing to sign in to and nothing to opt out of.
  • ✓
    Open source. The whole app is on GitHub under the GPL-3.0, including the build and the release process.
  • ✓
    Reproducible and signed. Each release is built twice by a public workflow to prove the result is identical, then signed. You can check the signature yourself below.
  • ✓
    Your backup is yours. Encrypted with AES-256-GCM and Argon2id, restored only with your passphrase.
