## 4. Derived Light Palettes

Two themes originally defined only as dark themes in community standards were derived into complementary light palettes:

### 1. Nord Light (Derived from Snow Storm)
- **Workspace screenshot:** `.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-17-emberbyte-redesign/palette-shots/nord_light.png`
- **Tokens & Derivation:**
  - Derived using the official Nord Snow Storm palette (`nord4` `#D8DEE9` to `nord6` `#ECEFF4`) for background and surface backgrounds, and Polar Night (`nord0` `#2E3440` to `nord3` `#4C566A`) for typography.
  - `primary`: Arctic blue `nord10` (`#5E81AC`), with `onPrimary` tuned to `#000000` for 5.1:1 contrast.
  - `accent`: Aurora sage `nord14` (`#A3BE8C`).
  - `track`: Tuned to `#DBE0E9` for 3.0:1 mark contrast.
- **Assessment:** Delivers a cool, calm Nordic winter ambiance with impeccable legibility and balanced contrast.

### 2. Dracula Light (Derived / "Alucard")
- **Workspace screenshot:** `.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-17-emberbyte-redesign/palette-shots/dracula_light.png`
- **Tokens & Derivation:**
  - Derived using Dracula's light companion specifications ("Alucard") with alabaster background `#F8F8F2` and parchment surface `#EAE8DC`.
  - `primary`: Dracula signature purple darkened to `#6242B6` for 5.0:1 contrast on white and alabaster.
  - `accent`: Deep emerald green `#1B8721` for 3.9:1 mark contrast.
  - `text`: Deep gothic charcoal `#282A36`.
- **Assessment:** Successfully preserves Dracula's recognizable purple-and-green identity in a readable day-mode palette without requiring any automatic adjustments.
