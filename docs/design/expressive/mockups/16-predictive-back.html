Predictive back, current vs refined

Press and drag from the left edge of either phone (the lilac handle), then let go. Past about a third of the way the back commits; before that it springs back. The right phone is the target look: the screen shrinks, rounds and follows your finger while the previous screen waits behind it.

Today (slide)
The old screen is dragged flat across the screen. No depth, no preview, no spring, and the previous screen is not visible until it ends.
Refined (Android 16 style)
Shrinks to 90%, corners round to 32 dp, slides 28 dp toward the finger, the screen behind scales up from 94% and brightens. Spring on release.

  • What the app does today. The NavHost reuses the tab slide for pop transitions, and the Plan editor, the new-plan flow and onboarding call BackHandler with enabled = true, which turns the system animation off and shows nothing while you swipe.
  • What Android gives us. Apps targeting Android 16 get predictive back by default. Navigation Compose 2.8+ drives pop transitions from the gesture progress, so replacing the pop transitions is enough for the in-app animation; the back-to-home swipe works as long as nothing intercepts back at the root.
  • Guarded screens. The editor and the flow only intercept back when the form is dirty, and then the swipe previews the screen shrinking, and on commit shows the "Discard changes?" dialog instead of leaving.
